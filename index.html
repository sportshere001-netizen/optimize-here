Universal Guide: How Tokens Work

AI models don't read words; they process text in chunks called Tokens (approx. 4 characters per token). Feeding an AI unoptimized data wastes tokens on spaces, line breaks, and conversational filler. This tool mathematically strips structural bloat while preserving your exact logical meaning, saving you API costs and expanding the AI's context window.

How the Logic Engine Works:

Unlike basic compressors, the "Smart Engine" utilizes an exclusion list. Conversational filler (like "basically", "actually") is removed, but vital structural constraints (like "no", "not", "all", "if", "then") are explicitly protected so the AI never misinterprets your prompt.
